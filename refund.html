Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

🛡️ 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee: If OmniTranslate Live does not meet your expectations, we will issue a 100% full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase — no questions asked.

1. 14-Day Refund Guarantee

We stand behind the quality of our real-time voice translation technology. If you are unsatisfied for any reason with your OmniTranslate Pro monthly or annual subscription, you are entitled to a full refund within 14 calendar days of your initial transaction.

2. How to Request a Refund

You can request a refund through either of the following methods:

  • Email Support: Send an email to support@omnitranslate.live with your purchase email address and order number. Refunds are processed within 1-2 business days.
  • Paddle Buyer Support: Since Paddle.com is our Merchant of Record, you can also request a refund directly through the link provided in your email receipt or via paddle.net.

3. Subscription Cancellation

You can cancel your recurring subscription at any time with zero cancellation fees. Following cancellation, your account will remain active with full Pro access until the conclusion of your current billing period, after which it will safely revert to the Free Starter tier without any further charges.

4. Chargebacks & Dispute Resolution

We encourage you to contact us directly at support@omnitranslate.live prior to filing a dispute or chargeback with your bank or credit card company. We resolve all legitimate refund requests promptly and amicably.
