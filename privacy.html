Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 18, 2026

At OmniTranslate Live, your privacy and data security are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, and protect your information.

1. Audio Data & Zero Retention Policy

OmniTranslate Live processes real-time audio strictly in-memory for translation synthesis. We do not record, store, or sell your raw audio files or transcripts on our servers. Audio segments are streamed transiently to Google Gemini AI API endpoints with encryption and are discarded immediately following translation synthesis.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register or purchase a subscription, we collect your email address and display name to manage licensing and sync quotas.
  • Payment Data: Payment details (credit cards, PayPal accounts, billing address) are collected and processed directly by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com. OmniTranslate Live never stores your credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Usage Metrics: We record aggregate metrics (e.g. number of translations performed, seconds elapsed) to enforce quota permissions and prevent abuse.

3. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted partners to deliver our services:

  • Paddle.com: Merchant of Record and payment processor.
  • Google Gemini AI: Neural translation and speech understanding models.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of your personal data at any time. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@omnitranslate.live.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to support@omnitranslate.live.
